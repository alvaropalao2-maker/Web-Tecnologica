Descripción del Producto

Step into the future of gaming with the ProX Gaming Console, designed for gamers who demand speed, performance, and immersive experiences. Whether you’re a casual gamer or a competitive esports player, the ProX delivers cutting-edge technology and seamless gameplay like never before.

El control de ganancia integrado permite ajustar el nivel de entrada en tiempo real. Compatible con OBS, Audacity, Adobe Audition y cualquier software de grabación — simplemente conecta y graba.
